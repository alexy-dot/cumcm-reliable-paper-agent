# Timed competition operations

Use a visible clock and preserve stage boundaries. Adjust exact minutes to the contest, but do not remove mandatory gates.

## Opening phase

- Freeze problem, attachments, official format, and rules before modeling.
- Complete question and data contracts.
- List ambiguities, simplest baselines, role ownership, and time budget.
- Stop if any required output, unit, hard constraint, or attachment schema remains unclear.

## First executable model

- Produce a minimal machine-readable result for every question.
- Add two anchors, unit checks, and constraint residuals.
- Keep all values provisional; preserve failed approaches.
- Stop if key numbers require copy-paste or the program depends on an undocumented local file.

## Pre-draft gate

- Complete P0 checks, independent reproduction, headline trace, and failure inventory.
- Reject leakage, formula-code drift, broken metric identities, and infeasible solutions.
- Draft only from verified results.

## Final freeze

- Re-run from frozen sources in a clean process.
- Rebuild tables, figures, paper, and support package.
- Validate current official format, anonymity, references, AI declaration, filenames, and uploads.
- Render and inspect the PDF page by page.
- Seal hashes only after all checks and required human sign-offs pass.

## Human sign-offs

Require named team approval for:

- central interpretation and ambiguity choice;
- main assumptions and model selection;
- at least one important numerical reproduction;
- final solution feasibility;
- abstract headlines and strong language;
- competition-rule and AI-use declaration.
